# AI Policy Writing – Vera Minder

## Who Loses Their Job? Digital Marginalisation and a Just AI Transition in the EU

Vera Minder, December 2025
Essay for the course "Artificial Intelligence: Law and Policy", Geneva Graduate Institute

**Question:** How does AI-driven automation produce digital marginalisation, and to what extent do existing legal frameworks for AI and work enable a just digital transition?

**Approach:** The essay applies the ILO just transition framework to AI-driven automation and analyses the EU AI Act, the GDPR, the Platform Work Directive and the European Pillar of Social Rights.

**Argument:** AI automation does not necessarily cause net job losses, but it distributes risks unevenly, mostly onto workers who are already disadvantaged. The EU should treat digital marginalisation as a governance issue and combine rights against automated discrimination with social dialogue, reskilling and redistribution.
